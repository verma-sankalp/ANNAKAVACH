Scientific Calculation Methodology

How Annakavach Matches Food Degradation Kinetics to Barrier Polymers

A transparent 4-stage algorithmic pipeline linking biochemical spoilage vectors directly to polymer thermodynamics and international engineering standards.

STAGE 01
Physico-Chemical Boundary Check
Evaluates water activity (aw), pH, lipid fraction, and active respiration against biological limits (C. botulinum pH 4.6 cutoff, pathogen aw 0.85 threshold, and anaerobic fermentation risk).
STAGE 02
Target Permeation Calculation
Applies Fick's first law of diffusion and Arrhenius kinetic rate laws to calculate target OTR and WVTR ranges required to protect product quality over the target distribution window.
STAGE 03
Multi-Layer Matching & EMA Modeling
Matches target flux against empirical ASTM D3985 / ASTM F1249 databases. For respiring produce, calculates laser micro-perforation density to achieve Equilibrium Modified Atmosphere (EMA).
STAGE 04
Circularity & Techno-Economics
Scores recyclability under EU PPWR 2030 design-for-recycling guidelines, calculates cradle-to-gate carbon footprint (ISO 14040), and models industrial converting economics per 1,000 units.
